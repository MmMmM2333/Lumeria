## Uses That Do Not Require Separate Permission

You may:

- truthfully refer to Lumeria when discussing, reviewing, or linking to the
  project;
- state that another project is compatible with, built from, or based on
  Lumeria, provided the statement is accurate and does not imply endorsement;
- retain factual copyright, attribution, and license notices;
- use the Lumeria name as needed to reproduce an unmodified official build,
  while clearly identifying its source.

Separate written permission is required to:

- use a Lumeria Mark as the name, logo, icon, domain, or primary branding of a
  different product, service, organization, or distribution;
- distribute a modified build in a way that could reasonably be mistaken for
  an official Lumeria release;
- imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or official status;
- use Lumeria branding in merchandise or commercial promotional material,
  except for truthful descriptive reference.

Forks and modified distributions may use the GPL-3.0-only-licensed source code,
but should adopt a distinct product name and replace the Lumeria logos,
application icons, and other brand assets. They may accurately describe
themselves as being based on Lumeria, provided they clearly state that they are
unofficial and are not endorsed by the Lumeria project.
